Neighborhood Overview

Verrado, Inside The City Of Buckeye

Where It Sits

Verrado is in the White Tank Mountain foothills, with Interstate 10 along its southern edge and Verrado Way as the main connection to the freeway.

What We Service

Detached homes, townhouses, and the multi-family buildings in the Main Street District, plus homes in the age-restricted Victory district.

Verrado is a master-planned community inside the city of Buckeye, about 25 miles from downtown Phoenix. Its own community website puts it at 8,800 acres. Construction began in the summer of 2002 and the first residents moved in during 2004, so Verrado now has homes from more than one building era.

The community is organized into named districts. Marketside District sits near the I-10 end and mixes detached homes with a built-to-rent villa community. The Main Street District is the walkable core, with shops, restaurants, and multi-family homes. Victory is the 55-plus district in the northern foothills.

Verrado's community FAQ says its water is supplied by EPCOR. Most other Buckeye addresses are billed by the City of Buckeye instead. If you are unsure which utility serves you, check your water statement before a service call, since it decides who to contact about a meter or main-line problem.

Good To Know

Verrado Address And ZIP Notes

Verrado addresses are most often listed under ZIP 85396, and some real-estate sources also show 85340. Treat the ZIP as a guide only, and tell us your street address when you call.

Practical Guidance

Practical Plumbing Guidance For Verrado Homes

Find Your Main Shutoff

Locate the main water shutoff before you need it. In an active leak, closing it stops the damage while you call.

Check Your Water Heater's Age

The serial number on the tank label usually encodes the manufacture date. Many tank-style heaters reach the end of their useful life around 8 to 12 years, though use and water conditions change that.

Shared Walls Change The Plan

If you live in a townhouse or a Main Street building, tell the technician early. Shared walls and shared lines affect where we can shut off water and who needs to be notified.
