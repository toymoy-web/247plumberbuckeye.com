General Plumbing Repair

For the plumbing problem that doesn't fit neatly into one category, we diagnose first and fix what's actually wrong.

View Service →

Most of what we see traces back to two regional realities: groundwater that runs hard across the entire West Valley, and slab-on-grade construction that's standard in nearly every home built here in the last two decades. Water heater and fixture repairs are usually a hard-water story. Slab leaks are usually a construction-type story. Knowing which one applies to your call changes how we approach the diagnosis before a technician ever arrives.

Every service below is diagnosed on-site before any work is quoted. If you're not sure which category your problem falls into, call and describe what's happening. We'll tell you what we think it is and what it typically involves before a technician is ever dispatched.
