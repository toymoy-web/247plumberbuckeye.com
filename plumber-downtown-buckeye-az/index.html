Neighborhood Overview

Downtown Buckeye, The Original Townsite

Where It Sits

The original town was built around Monroe Avenue, the main street of downtown, with the railroad and the old business district nearby.

What We Service

Houses, shops, offices, and small businesses in and around the downtown blocks.

Downtown Buckeye is the original town center, built around Monroe Avenue. The town was founded in 1888 as Sidney, renamed Buckeye in 1910, and incorporated in 1929. The Arizona Eastern Railroad arrived in 1910 and shaped where the business district grew.

Several historic buildings still stand in and near downtown. The Buckeye Women's Club on East Monroe Avenue was built in 1935 and listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2021. The Old Courthouse and Jail is on South 4th Street, and Buckeye Union High School's A-Wing on East Eason Avenue dates to 1925.

Residential streets run alongside the business blocks, so this part of Buckeye mixes houses, small shops, and offices. We cannot tell a building's plumbing from its age alone, so we start with a camera inspection or a pressure check instead of assuming what is behind the walls.

The City of Buckeye provides water to most residents, according to its utility billing page. The Utility Billing Office at 623-349-6100 can confirm which provider serves your address.

A first visit downtown is mostly about information. The technician looks at the fixture or line, asks what you have noticed, and explains the options in plain terms. You approve the price before any repair work starts, and if the problem turns out to be small, we say so instead of selling a bigger job.
