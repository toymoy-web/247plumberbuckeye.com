Neighborhood Overview

Sundance, On Buckeye's West Side

Where It Sits

Sundance is on Buckeye's west side, in the area between Interstate 10 and State Route 85, with Sundance Towne Center at Watson Road and Yuma Road.

What We Service

Single-family homes in Sundance's named neighborhoods, plus businesses in and around the Towne Center.

Sundance is a master-planned community on the west side of Buckeye, in the area between Interstate 10 and State Route 85. Sundance Towne Center, the community's shopping center, sits at the corner of Watson Road and Yuma Road south of I-10. Its first phase opened in spring 2007.

Real-estate records list several named neighborhoods inside Sundance, including Village at Sundance and Sierra and Highland at Sundance, all single-family. That means most calls here involve houses rather than apartment buildings, and the work is usually everyday repair rather than major renovation.

The community has been in place since the 2000s, so some homes are well past their first appliance cycle while other streets are newer. We treat each call on its own facts. A technician checks the age and condition of the fixture before recommending repair or replacement, and you approve a clear price before work starts.

The City of Buckeye provides water to most residents, while EPCOR and Arizona Water Company serve parts of the area. If you are not sure which applies to your address, the city's utility billing office at 623-349-6100 can confirm it.

Practical Guidance

Practical Plumbing Guidance For Sundance Homes

One Fixture Or The Whole House

Low pressure at a single faucet usually points to that fixture, while low pressure everywhere points to the supply side. Telling us which you see speeds up the diagnosis.

Slow Drains Start Small

A drain that is slow in one sink is usually a local clog. If several drains slow down together, the problem may be in the main line, and that is when to call.

Know Your Valves

Learn where the main shutoff and the water heater shutoff are. Knowing both lets you stop a leak quickly while you wait for help.
