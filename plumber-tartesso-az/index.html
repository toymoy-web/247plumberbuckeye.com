Tartesso, A Long-Term Buckeye Build-Out

Where It Sits

Tartesso is in Buckeye and is reached from Sun Valley Parkway.

What We Service

Single-family homes in Tartesso's builder neighborhoods, from early-phase streets to newer construction.

Tartesso is a large master-planned development in Buckeye, reached from Sun Valley Parkway. Public records describe it as the third-largest development in Buckeye, with more than 10,000 acres and a build-out that may take about 25 years.

It was first announced in 2005. An early first phase had 964 of its 3,375 lots occupied by January 2008. The land changed hands in 2016, D.R. Horton's Express Homes opened a new subdivision in late 2016, and the first closings followed in January 2017. A single Tartesso street can therefore be much newer than the next one over.

Newer homes usually come with a builder warranty, and the terms vary by builder and by what failed. Before you call anyone about a problem on a recent build, read your warranty paperwork so you know what the builder covers.
